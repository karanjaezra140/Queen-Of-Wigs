Who We Are

Beauty That Empowers

Queen of Wigs is a modern beauty brand dedicated to providing high-quality human hair wigs and stylish hair solutions, based in Naivasha, Kenya. Our mission is to help women express their beauty and confidence through premium wigs.

We carefully select and style every wig to ensure it is fashionable, comfortable, and long-lasting. Whether you want curly, straight, or lace wigs, Queen of Wigs has a style for every personality.

Founded with passion and a deep understanding of what women want, we have grown to serve over 500 happy customers across Kenya — and we're just getting started. We now offer seamless M-Pesa checkout so you can shop from anywhere in Kenya.
